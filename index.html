Model equations and assumptions

Model class: reduced-order, lumped-parameter transient thermal model with a simplified coolant-flow and radiator effectiveness relationship.

Brake power proxyPbrake = 60 × (RPM / 2000) × Load kW
Heat to coolantQengine = 0.32 × Pbrake kW
Coolant flowFlow = (28 + 0.006 × RPM) × PumpHealth L/min
Radiator heat rejectionQrad = UAeff × max(0, Tmean − Tambient) kW
Thermal storageCth × dT/dt = Qengine − Qrad − Qother
Model residualrT = Tplant,measured − Ttwin,predicted

Initial placeholders: coolant specific heat 3.6 kJ/kg·K; lumped thermal capacity 180 kJ/K; nominal radiator UA 0.17 kW/K. These are demonstration parameters only. They require OEM data, measured pump/fan maps and test-bench calibration before representing a specific engine.

Diagnostic philosophy: data-quality validation → independent twin prediction → residual calculation → fault hypothesis. Severity and confidence are reported separately. This prototype implements initial deterministic signatures, not a validated diagnostic classifier.

Acceptance test notes
  • Normal operation: plant and healthy twin should track closely.
  • Higher load: engine heat input should increase.
  • Pump degradation: flow residual should become negative and thermal deviation should develop.
  • Fan, radiator and thermostat faults: plant temperature should diverge upward from the healthy twin.
  • Sensor bias/dropout: diagnostic layer should identify measurement deviation or bad quality without treating missing data as zero.
